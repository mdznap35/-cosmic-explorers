# Product brief — Cosmic Explorers

## Audience and job

An Arabic-first astronomy discovery experience for a young child who is beginning to read. The adult is the setup and supervision partner. The child's primary interaction is touch, exploration, listening, and observing.

## Product promise

A calm, beautiful, hands-on journey through space that makes one idea understandable at a time.

## Experience principles

1. Show before explaining: a visual action should introduce each new idea.
2. One learning objective per moment: avoid dense cards and long paragraphs.
3. Audio is optional, never required to finish an activity.
4. Every screen has an obvious way back.
5. Reward curiosity and exploration, not speed or streak pressure.
6. No advertisements, external links, chat, account, or analytics in the child experience.
7. Work offline after installation.

## First release scope

- Sun and eight major planets in a navigable 3D scene.
- Tap a planet in the scene or choose it from the planet strip.
- Camera focus, reset, and simple zoom controls.
- Short Arabic descriptions and three facts per planet.
- Local-only discovery progress.
- Landscape Android APK, arm64-v8a, Compatibility renderer.

## Explicit non-goals for round one

- Exact ephemerides or scale-accurate distances.
- Real-time NASA feeds or internet calls.
- Voice-over recordings, full mascot animation, or complex physics experiments.
- Cloud accounts, leaderboards, social sharing, or parental data collection.

## Learning loop

Notice a planet → choose it → move closer → hear/read one clear fact → reveal more facts → choose another world.

## Visual direction

Deep navy space, restrained cyan navigation accents, warm gold focus states, large touch targets, rounded glass-like panels, and procedural 3D celestial bodies. Keep contrast high and motion calm. UI text must remain legible over the scene.
